# Travelling in Future

Travelling in Future is an interactive web project that lets you explore what travelling might look like in the future.

You can choose a **year, destination, and type of transport**, then start a simulated journey. After arriving, you can explore different locations and learn about technologies that could be possible in that future.

## What you can do

* Choose between **2035, 2050, and 2100**
* Travel to a **Future City, the Moon, or Mars**
* Choose different types of transport
* Watch the journey progress from Earth to your destination
* Explore different points of interest after arriving
* Scan locations to unlock information
* Earn **XP** for discoveries
* See whether an idea is **REALISTIC, POSSIBLE, or SPECULATIVE**

## Why I made it

I wanted to make a project about the future of travelling that feels more like an experience than a normal information website.

Instead of just reading about future technology, you can choose where and when you want to travel and then explore that world yourself.

I also wanted to separate ideas that are already realistic from ideas that are possible or still speculative.

## Built with

* React
* TypeScript
* Vite
* CSS

## Project structure

The main experience has three parts:

**Home** — Plan your journey by choosing the year, destination, and transport.

**Travel** — Watch your simulated journey from Earth to the selected destination.

**Explore** — Investigate locations, read about the technology, and collect discoveries.

## Note

Some of the future locations and technologies in this project are speculative. They are included to encourage thinking about what future travel could become, rather than to predict exactly what will happen.
